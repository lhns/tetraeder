# Box session driver brief (read fully before any taxameter call)

You drive ONE Vast box for ONE session profile of Tetraeder and bring back committed, verified results. Money is real: the box bills every minute.

## Read first
- AGENTS.md, sections "GPU work on Vast.ai" and "Benchmark comparability on Vast", in full. Every rule applies: key hygiene (never print or pass ~/.config/vastai/vast_api_key), --id on every command, detached flock-guarded launches, waits on the box with `ssh --timeout 540`, no inline pgrep/pkill patterns in ssh, never overwrite a running script, `cuda: ok` before launch, destroy after a verified fetch, `date +%T` at launch and checks.
- docs/sessions.md: your profile's box requirements, stages, time estimates, launch command, verify and fetch steps.
- scripts/session.sh (SESSION, DEADLINE, RESULTS_DATE, RESUME), taxameter.toml.

## Caps (do not raise; never edit taxameter.toml caps)
- Per box: the max price in your task; 3 h box life unless your task says otherwise; the script budget in docs/sessions.md.
- At most two boxes for the project; another driver runs the other box. Never act on a box you did not create. Run `uv run taxameter status` before `create`; if two boxes are up, wait and report.
- Check CPU model, cores, host RAM and disk bandwidth before `create`: see "Machine choice" below. Slow disks and low-quota hosts cut stages short.
- `taxameter adopt <id>` takes the id as a positional argument (there is no `--id`); `taxameter status` must then show the box as `managed:active` before you run anything on it.
- Sessions that fetch their own model (b1-exit does, docs/sessions.md) provision with `--models ,` so provisioning downloads nothing.
- b1-exit and b1b-levers: set `.vast/config.json` to `{"disk_gb": 230}` before `create` (git-ignored; the 235B needs it). The RAM budget defaults to 91 GB in these profiles (`EMULATE_RAM_GB`); check the exit_run.log start line says `memory 91000000000 B` before the download ends.
- Pick offers by cores and traffic price: `uv run taxameter offers --min-cpu-cores <n> --download-gb <gb>` with the GB your session fetches, then `create` with the same flags and your price cap.

## Machine choice (standing policy, owner 2026-11-13: drivers pick, nobody asks)
The driver picks the box by these rules and does not ask the owner. The coordinator asks the owner only for spend above the ceiling, credit below one session, or a verdict.

1. **Hard requirements, checked on the offer** (`uv run taxameter offers ...`, whose price is all-in with disk; `vast_offers.py` is for CPU facts only, its listed price has twice been under the real one):
   - hourly price with disk at most the ceiling in your task (the owner's cap is per box per hour); the model download traffic (`dl$/GB` x 145 GB) counts against the session budget in your task, not the hourly cap (default 0.90 USD/h, taxameter.toml);
   - disk at least 230 GB and 5 GB/s (ADR 0045), at least 24 GB VRAM, host not blocklisted (also avoid 269862: 15 to 25 MB/s downloads on 13d and 13e);
   - `memory.max` at least 150 GB, so the 91 GB budget is set by the balloon as on `20261113b` (check after SSH; the offer's RAM column can understate it). With `memory.max` near 91 GB the llama.cpp cache thrashed mmap and gave no ratio (`20261113g`);
   - point 1 (llama.cpp cache): Zen 2/3 EPYC, quota at least 24, so both engines run 24 threads without going over quota;
   - point 3 (KTransformers): GPU compute capability 8.0, 8.6, 8.9 or 9.0 (the kt-kernel wheel has no other; a Blackwell RTX 5090, 12.0, cost 13h its KT stages); AVX-512 with VNNI and VBMI (/proc/cpuinfo flags `avx512_vnni` and `avx512vbmi`, no underscore in the second; 13i misread a Zen 4 as lacking it); prefer CPUs without AMX (Zen 4, Ice Lake), where KT's own pick is its avx512 kernel; threads follow `scripts/kt_threads.sh` (quota minus 4: KT busy-spins its workers and was throttled 32% at quota minus 2 on 13d). Stages: without AMX, `ktransformers` and `ktransformers-repeat` (its own pick is its best kernel); with AMX, `ktransformers-avx512` and its repeat interleaved with the builder, then `ktransformers` (amx); the faster KT kernel is the baseline.
2. **Ranking:** lowest session cost = hourly price x planned hours + traffic (`dl$/GB` x 145 GB for the model). At 0.013 USD/GB the model alone costs 1.9 USD.
3. **Fit before create:** download (at 100 MB/s, about 25 min) plus the measured stage budgets (`scripts/stage_budget.sh`) must end by box age 2h25m. Drop stages from B1_STAGES up front rather than letting the deadline guard cut the one that matters.
4. **No qualifying offer:** report and stop; the coordinator rechecks the offers every 30 minutes and starts a new driver when one appears. Do not relax a requirement to fill the slot.
5. **Abort gates** (destroy after fetching what exists, then try the next offer once):

The power gate (`scripts/power_gate.sh <a>,<b>`) judges only the pair; the other GPUs are listed as not judged. All other abort gates stay. The script exports `CUDA_DEVICE_ORDER=PCI_BUS_ID` and `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES`; run it once with the pair and do not mix pairs inside one session (a rerun resumes with the same `P5_DEVICES`);
   - status `loading` (image pull) or SSH refused for more than 10 minutes after create (13f lost 40 minutes to three such boxes: host 213498 rejected the key on direct and proxy ports, host 546840 never finished the pull; skip both; host 109790 stayed loading twice on 13j and host 39788 once on 13l, skip both; hosts 151822, 130210, 51345, 551866, 87657 and 70221, 305845, 116365, 393491 and 92795 never finished the pull or SSH, 166946 and 450573 rejected the key on 2026-11-17, 569309, 142 and 443829 stayed loading and 451840 pulled models at 75 KB/s on 2026-11-19 (4-GPU offers, s3d), 208874 and 167329 stayed loading on 2026-11-20 (2-GPU offers at `--min-inet 500`, V2), 151822 stalled twice and 608696 had both GPUs at 300 W against 350 W on 2026-11-21 (V2b), 685226 (all four GPUs at 250 to 300 W against 350) and 399360 (all four at 320 W against 350) on 2026-11-21 (V4a), 208874 again on 2026-11-21 (4-GPU offer, V4a; second stall, still loading after 10 min, retry per the stall rule), 203531 never opened SSH and 611393 and 706255 had both GPUs below their default power limit on 2026-11-21 (V2b; 706255 at 280 W against 390 and 420); 397236 stalled once, then both GPUs at 300 W against 350, V2b 2026-11-21; 87485 ran V2 2026-11-20 with a busy co-tenant (19 to 27 host cores outside the container) and 0x20 on both GPUs, so no verdict (all slow-network offers except 87485): pick offers with `--min-inet 1000` and skip them);

## Known box pitfalls (from b3-detector, 2026-11-10; follow these until the scripts are fixed)
- vendor/llama.cpp and bench/results are not pushed (main bd9933e): the provision hook fetches llama.cpp at the pin and applies the patch queue (`scripts/ensure_patches.sh`). A push over about 5 MB means the config regressed; stop and report.
- Prebuilt test binaries run directly (e.g. by detector_campaign.sh) need `LD_LIBRARY_PATH` including the ggml-sys build's `out/lib` (find it under target/*/build/tetraeder-ggml-sys-*/out/lib on the box) when relaunching.
- Logs append across relaunches: wait loops must check the LAST line (`tail -n1`) for `*_DONE` markers, not grep the whole file.
- `kernels-cpu` has no resume: do not relaunch it if its results exist.

- co-tenant gate: measure with `scripts/engine_sample.py` (its host view counts iowait as idle, `engine_sample.py:357`), never an ad-hoc `/proc/stat` sum; on 2026-11-24 an ad-hoc count that took iowait as busy rejected host 254084 at 60 cores where the sampler would have read under 1. Skip hosts 570868 (rejected the SSH key) and 169171 (image pull stalled twice, 2026-11-24).

## Flow
1. You are in an isolated worktree. `git fetch origin && git checkout -B <your-branch> origin/main`. taxameter pushes the committed HEAD of this worktree to the box; keep HEAD = origin/main until the results commit.
2. `date +%T`; `uv run taxameter status`; `offers`; `create` with the real download size: traffic counts toward the per-box cap (AGENTS.md "Money", owner 2026-11-24), so every `offers` and `create` carries `--download-gb` (about 150 for the 235B; prefer hosts with cheap traffic) and the all-in price including traffic must be within the cap. For the segment confirmation use `bash scripts/sc_offers.sh offers ...` and `bash scripts/sc_offers.sh create --offer <id> ...`, which add `--download-gb ${DOWNLOAD_GB:-150}` and `--max-price`, refuse a hand-typed value and write `{"disk_gb": 230}` into `.vast/config.json` (`DISK_GB`, at least 230; taxameter has no `create` option for the disk, and the 80 GB of `taxameter.toml` filled up on `segment-confirm-20261125b`; `DRY_RUN=1` writes nothing) (record the id; a job that needs open ports passes `create --ports 7000,7001/udp` and reads the mapping from `endpoint`, taxameter v0.3.6 ADR 0014; never patch or wrap taxameter); `provision --id <id>` with the models your profile needs (docs/sessions.md); the provision log must say `cuda: ok` or `cuda: ok_preload`, else destroy and report.
2b. Judged confirmation sessions (`scripts/sc_box.sh`), right after SSH and before the model download: `STAGES=gates RESULTS_NAME=<name> bash scripts/sc_box.sh` (via `taxameter run` or the detached ssh fallback), then read the last lines of its log. The stage runs `power_gate.sh` and `idle_gate.sh` first (output in `env/power-gate.txt`, `env/idle-gate.txt`), then needs `lscpu` to show exactly one NUMA node and `engine_sample.py cotenant` to read under `cotenant_guard`'s limit, and ends `SC_BOX_DONE rc=1` otherwise: destroy the box, nothing was downloaded, try the next offer. Never replace the co-tenant reading with an ad-hoc sum (2026-11-24 did, read 22.5 over 19.2, and went ahead): the judged stages recheck both from `env/lscpu.txt` and `env/cotenant.json` and refuse. Start the download with `REQUIRE_ONE_NUMA=1 bash scripts/fetch_exit_model.sh`, which refuses a multi-node host itself. The session then runs `STAGES="env build disk balloon trace anchors predict"`, you commit the prediction (a fit band over 0.15 or an anchor on fixed-seed input leaves nothing frozen: report and destroy), then `STAGES="judged aalast" FROZEN_SHA=<FROZEN.sha256>`. After judged round 2 the early A/A runs; `SC_BOX_DONE rc=1` there means outside the 0.08 band on T: fetch, destroy, report, do not run on. `aalast` must be the last stage.
3. Launch: `uv run taxameter run --id <id> scripts/session.sh --env SESSION=<profile> [other --env from your task]`. Check the session log advances within a few minutes; if `run` died silently, use the detached ssh fallback from docs/sessions.md.
4. Wait on the box with `uv run taxameter ssh --id <id> --timeout 540 -- '<wait loop>'`; between waits compare progress with the estimates and flag overruns. No tight local polling loops (the local machine is low on memory and reaps background shells).
5. At the session's done marker (or the deadline): `fetch --id <id>` (`destroy` needs `bench/results/<name>/DONE`: `sc_box.sh` writes it at the end of every run and after an abort (`SC_BOX_DONE rc=N`); `taxameter.toml` `[results] include` carries `*.toml`, `*.sha256` and `SHA256SUMS`, so the frozen prediction comes back with the fetch; if an older taxameter skips them, copy `predictions/` and `trace/*.sha256` over ssh and run `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS` in the results dir and in `predictions/` for `FROZEN.sha256`), verify the expected files per docs/sessions.md "Verify", then `destroy --id <id> --yes`. Never destroy before a verified fetch unless the box is already gone.
6. Commit the fetched results (bench/results/<date>/..., JSON and small logs; no GGUF; no traces over a few MB) plus `bench/results/<session>/README.md` (first lines: status as clean, unclean or null with the reason, the rule it was judged under, commit, box id, host, GPU, driver; then the findings: the numbers of your report, checked against verdict.json or the result files) on your branch, message naming the session and box id; push the branch. Never push to main.
7. Report: box id, host, GPU, CPU cores/quota, price, wall time, cost (box plus traffic from `inet_down_billed`/`inet_down_cost`), stages run and skipped, key numbers per exit test (paired ratios on this box, not absolute tok/s across boxes), failures and why, result file paths, branch.

## On surprises
- A stage fails: read its log on the box; fix only a trivial env or path issue, with a new script name (never overwrite a running script), and note it; otherwise let the session continue and report.
- Diagnostic session scripts are fail-soft: a failed gate is recorded and only the stages that depend on it are skipped; verdict sessions keep the hard stop (ADR 0068). Briefs name the expected failures and the reaction (for example: a non-parity gate test fails, relaunch the session script once with its skip knob).
- A gate, test or stage fails on a box that works and the brief has no answer, or a command is denied: keep the box, without leaving it idle longer than this wait. Stop the work on it and send the coordinator (SendMessage) what failed, the evidence and a proposed next step, then wait up to 15 minutes for an answer, as an on-box wait (`ssh --timeout 540 -- 'sleep 300'`), not a local poll. No answer: fetch what exists, verify, destroy, report. If the answer has to come from the user (a permission denial), fetch and `taxameter stop --id <id>` instead: the disk, the build and the models survive at storage rates; the GPU may be rented away meanwhile. taxameter counts `max_box_hours` from the provider's start time (`box_age_h`), so stopped hours may count; after a `start`, check `status` for the age, and past the cap `run` refuses new work: ask the coordinator. A box idle for 15 minutes costs about 0.30 USD; a new box costs its setup again (`s3f-20261120`: a gate failure on an unrelated timing test threw away 55 minutes, about 1.2 USD).
- An image pull or SSH not up after 10 minutes: destroy, then re-create once on the same host if it still fits; skip it only after a second stall. One stall is not a host property (20210 stalled once and then ran three sessions; 744195 stalled at `--min-inet 1000`). Capped power and rejected keys are host properties: skip at once.
- Host faults have nothing to resume, so destroy at once: no `cuda: ok`, a power limit below default, an image pull stalled past 10 minutes, the abort gates above.
- Anything that risks money (box unreachable, GPU idle, provisioning loops, a cap about to be hit): fetch what exists, destroy, report.
- Use the advisor when stuck before improvising.

## Phase 6 exit sessions (ADR 0091 Case F and Case S; ADR 0092 rerun rules)

Two boxes per case, one driver for both (`--id` on every call, which the scripts do). The scripts run on the driver machine and reach the boxes through `taxameter ssh`, `put` and `create --ports 7000,7001` (7000 the cluster, 7001 the Case F fault trigger; the box reads its public address and the mapped ports from `/proc/1/environ`, `phase6_box.sh endpoint [PORT]`). Box spec: one RTX 3090, CPU quota 8 or more, within 0.90 USD/h all-in each, both `cuda: ok`; a pair with a small-frame round trip under 5 ms first, else any two hosts. The summaries are `scripts/phase6_exit_f_summary.py` and `scripts/phase6_exit_s_summary.py`; every pass rule is a named line of `rules` in `verdict-f.json` / `verdict-s.json`, and missing evidence fails the rule.

**Gates, on both boxes, before any download** (`phase6_box.sh gates`, rechecked on the driver from the committed files): power limit at default, idle gate, `lscpu` shows one NUMA node, the co-tenant reading of `engine_sample.py cotenant` under `cotenant_guard`'s limit. A miss ends the session: destroy the box, nothing was downloaded, try the next offer. Create the boxes with `create --ports 7000,7001` (`sc_offers.sh create --offer ID --ports 7000` for Case S, which has no trigger: real `--download-gb 150`, `disk_gb` 230). Write `cluster.toml` only after both exist (its creation epoch is a rule); the two `T_CREATED_*` epochs are env of the driver.

**Pair finder, before renting for Case S** (`scripts/phase6_pair_finder.py`; advisor rows of 2026-11-26 in `docs/notes/phase6-rule-changes.md`). Run `uv run python scripts/phase6_pair_finder.py` (optionally `TETRAEDER_PLAN_BIN=target/release/tetraeder-plan`; `--out F` writes the JSON, `--save-offers F` records the offers for a fixture). It reads the market only: three searches per GPU class through taxameter's own provider (the same query as `offers`; its `--json` output has no RAM, disk, inet or datacenter fields, and offer ids rotate between two searches), merged by machine and price. **Create both boxes together, and only when it lists a pair (exit 0); never hold one box for a partner.** On "NO PAIR" create nothing, read the per-rule counts it prints (first failing rule, and how many offers fail each rule alone), retry later and never loosen a rule. Rules per offer: one RTX 3090, A40, RTX A6000, L40 or L40S (a pair with an A-class card is listed as not plannable, and exit 1 if only those exist, because the cluster profile cannot cap or price them yet: node `cluster-profile-gpu-cap`; `--plan-aclass` lifts it once it lands); missing RAM, disk, inet or traffic data is counted as `unknown`, not guessed; host not on the skip list (the stall list above plus 269862, taxameter's blocklist 366851 and 483833, and from the 2026-11-26 pairs: 36536 power capped, 44173 key rejected and two sockets, 568479, 546840 and 281790 two sockets; 682584, the only A40 host seen, two sockets and two NUMA nodes; from 2026-11-27: 392144 power capped at 360 of 370 W); at least 12 usable cores; container RAM at least 61.7 GB; disk at least 230 GB; inet down at least 1000 Mbit/s; all-in price at most 0.90 USD/h with 150 GB of traffic priced over the 3 h session; the two offers on different hosts. **RAM floor:** the heavier node of the covering pick needs 57.1 GB of host budget; under the 64 GB balloon the budget is 64 GB minus the box's own use (4.6 GB at the 59.4 GB the pick was made on; 4.2 and 2.9 GB measured on 2026-11-26), and a box under 64 GB runs without the balloon on its real limit minus the same use, so the floor is 57.1 + 4.6 = 61.7 GB (the old 80 GB rule asked for more than the budget rule needs); from 64 GB up the balloon fixes the budget at 59.4 GB. Power limit, NUMA nodes and sockets are not in the offer: the gates on the box decide them (the finder flags them). Pairs are ranked first by how many of their hosts passed the on-box gates of an earlier Case S pair (`GATED_HOSTS`: 79466 and 45726; a ranking preference only, the gates still run; advisor, delegated by the owner 2026-11-27), then by expected bulk rate (the slower direction of upload and download, 500 Mbit/s buckets capped at 4000), then datacenter hosts, then price; RTT is not ranked. With a built planner it also runs `candidates --cluster --covering` per pair on a two-node graph from the committed WAN fixture with each offer's RAM budget and cores, and drops a pair with no covering plan; without one it reports the static pass only. The GPU-class priors of the Rust lane are not landed, so the 3090 prior stands in.

**Case F** (`scripts/phase6_exit_f.sh`, 18.6 GB model, no 142 GB download): `RESULTS_NAME=<session> ID_A=.. ID_B=.. T_CREATED_A=.. T_CREATED_B=.. bash scripts/phase6_exit_f.sh` runs `gates setup trials summary`. Budget per box: about 20 min of setup (build, 18.6 GB download), then nine trials of about 5 min each (warm-up until B serves, 180 s of load, the fault, the restore, the load carried on to restore + 60 s), so about 1.2 h and 1.5 USD per box with the traffic (rehearsal: 1.83 USD for both boxes and three aborted ones). The load runs on box A against the head (closed loop, concurrency 8, streaming, prompt sized through `/tokenize`, 256 output tokens). **Fault timing** (advisor, delegated by the owner, 2026-11-25; `phase6_trigger.py`): `phase6_box.sh trigger` starts a small service on B (own process, port 7001, a random token written to both boxes by the driver, a fixed list of three faults); `phase6_load.py` on A waits `FAULT_AT` seconds, polls the trigger until B's own log (the newest `live=N` of a `tetraeder::step` line, at most 0.5 s old) shows requests in flight on B, and fires the fault over that direct connection. The trigger delivers the signal and then answers, so the fault time is the middle of send and ack on A's clock, uncertain by half that interval (tens of ms on a WAN); the answer carries B's reading (`fault.live_b`). B's clock against A's is read the same way (20 direct exchanges, the narrowest kept, recorded per trial with its uncertainty), not through `taxameter ssh` (2 to 6 s), and only moves B's restore and admit epochs onto A's clock; the 0.5 s rule on it stays. **Invalid attempts:** an attempt whose fault found `live_b` 0 hit no request in flight on B; it is repeated, at most twice per mode (`MAX_REPEATS`), and every attempt is kept in `trials/<mode>-<attempt>/` and listed in `verdict-f.json` (`attempts`). The reading is B's, never the client's outcome, so a real failure cannot be relabelled invalid; missing evidence fails `fault_evidence`; a mode with fewer than three valid attempts is incomplete and more than two invalid ones fail `repeats_within_limit`. Trial 1 is the first valid attempt. Restore epochs: the daemon's new child (kill of the engine), the daemon start (kill of the daemon), the SIGCONT (stop). B counts as serving at its first `admit` line at or after the restore, searched only up to restore + 60 s. The script never restarts the head or a node: when B is not serving before a trial it stops; fix it by hand, record it with `bash scripts/phase6_exit_f.sh note "<what>"` (trial 1 of a mode fails its rule when an action falls between its fault and its rejoin deadline) and run the stage again; finished trials are kept. The verdict is `pass` only when every rule holds in all nine trials; the rule list is the header of `phase6_exit_f_summary.py`. Mode 1 is the kill of the replica engine, mode 2 of the daemon and its children, mode 3 SIGSTOP and SIGCONT after 60 s.

**Case S** (`scripts/phase6_exit_s.sh`, 142 GB model on both boxes): two invocations with a commit between them. `STAGES="gates bulk setup dryrun budget plan linkgate fetch calib predict"` ends with a frozen prediction (`predictions/FROZEN.sha256`); commit and push `bench/results/<session>/` (predictions, link, plans, calib, trace hashes), then `FROZEN_SHA=<hash> STAGES="judged aalast linkend summary report"`. **Order** (advisor, delegated by the owner, 2026-11-25): everything that needs the balloon and the committed layout but not the model comes before the download, so a refused or non-covering pick costs no 142 GB. `budget` (`sc_box.sh env balloon`: the RAM budget under the balloon, no model), `plan` (the committed layout `bench/results/segment-confirm-20261125b/layout.json`, a probe, `cluster profile --ram-budget`, then `tetraeder-plan candidates --cluster --covering` and the pick; no covering plan that computes on both nodes stops the session here), `linkgate`, and only then `fetch`. After the fetch `tetraeder cluster model-id --install` runs on both boxes (detached: it hashes five shards, `phase6_box.sh model-id`), the two ids must agree, and `calib` starts with `sc_box.sh disk` (the model's own layout, which must equal the committed one, the NVMe curve, the graph seeded with the measured budget) and the start probe. **Covering pick** (advisor, delegated by the owner, 2026-11-25; `docs/notes/phase6-rule-changes.md`): judged candidates are covering segments only, every node's VRAM plus RAM slots hold all 128 experts of its layers and fit the measured budgets and 24 GiB (`enumerate_cluster_covering`); each node's V and R are the pick's own (`plans/slots.txt`), the anchors run them, the workers run them, and the summary rule `segments_covering` checks both (`slots` of the old global V and R is gone). On two 3090 nodes at 59.4 GB the pick is 0..44 on a and 44..94 on b, 32 + 96 slots each (b: 57.1 GB host and 23.8 GB VRAM by the planner's estimate), covering; at 40 GB no covering candidate exists and the planner names the need. **Link gate before the download** (stage `linkgate`, summary rule `link_gate`): the build and the daemons come first (`setup`, build before the certificates), then two full probes (frames to 64 MiB, no `--max-bytes`; a probe that dies aborts the pair, there is no truncated fallback) at least 10 minutes apart (`LINK_GATE_WAIT_S`, never below 600). The plan that is priced is the pick itself (`plans/judged-plan.toml`), on the plan's graph (`plans/hw-plan.json`: the measured RAM budgets, the discovered builder disk, the plan stage's probe) with only the link curves swapped to each gate probe (`cluster merge --swap-link`), uniform routing, no replay; the rule compares the plan hash of both prices with the pick's. Pp or tg moving by more than 0.08 aborts the pair: destroy both boxes, nothing was downloaded (`fetch` refuses without a passed gate). The frozen start probe is taken after the fetch (`link/probe-start.json`, swapped into the plan's graph as `link/hw.json`): a start probe from before the download would be an hour old at the first round.
